// Capabilities

What this lab does with media evidence

Each capability below runs on verified working copies. The original file is hashed, locked read-only, and never touched again until the matter closes.

01

RING VERITAS™

Doorbell & security-camera footage recovery

  • Repairs corrupted MP4 headers from Ring, Tapo, and similar consumer camera footage
  • Extracts lossless 16-bit PCM audio and renders full-spectrum linear spectrograms
  • Flags clipping, DC offset, and AGC artifacts before analysis begins
02

AUDACITYBLOCK™

Audio authenticity & speaker verification

  • 60 Hz electrical network frequency (ENF) phase-continuity analysis flags likely splice points
  • F0 pitch and F1/F2 vocal formant tracking supports speaker identification
  • Findings compiled into a multi-page report formatted for attorney and court review
03

Media Authenticity & Chain-of-Custody Verification

Is this file what it claims to be?

  • SHA-256 hashing performed before and after every analysis step
  • Encoder/editing-software fingerprint detection flags footage re-rendered through editing tools
  • Group-of-Pictures frame-structure verification checks for re-encoding inconsistent with raw capture
04

AI-Assisted Video & Audio Review

Finding the frames and moments that matter

  • Automated person and vehicle detection/tracking across long-form footage
  • Facial-region sharpness scoring identifies the clearest usable frames
  • Transcription of audio tracks, including degraded or low-volume recordings
05

Editing-Project Reconstruction

Recovering what an edited export leaves out

  • Parses professional video-editing project files directly, without needing the original software
  • Recovers linked source clips, timeline trim points, and original file paths
  • Useful when a client or opposing party has only produced an edited export, not the raw capture
06

Camera Stream Security Auditing

Network-level support for authenticity findings

  • Evaluates whether a camera's live audio stream is encrypted end-to-end or exposed to local tampering
  • Checks for known signaling and encryption weaknesses in the captured network traffic
  • Adds network-level evidence alongside file-level authenticity findings
// Methodology

How evidence moves through the lab

01

Intake & Hash

The original media is hashed on arrival and locked read-only before anything else happens.

02

Non-Destructive Analysis

All repair, extraction, and detection work happens on verified working copies — never the original.

03

Documentation

Every step is logged and re-hashed, so the chain of custody can be reconstructed and defended.

04

Reporting

Findings are compiled into a structured report for your attorney, insurer, or investigator to use.

WHAT WE DO NOT DO

We do not alter or edit evidentiary media, and we do not render opinions on guilt, liability, or credibility. Our reports document what the data shows — authenticity, repair, and recovery — for the legal professional handling your matter to use as they see fit.
